The problem

Our neighbors are going without solutions that shouldn't be hard to find

Elderly and disabled residents in Forsyth County need small custom physical solutions every day — a grab bar that fits their specific wall, a ramp that matches their doorway, a handle their arthritic hands can grip. These devices either don't exist in standard sizes or cost hundreds of dollars. Most people go without — increasing their risk of falls, injury, and loss of independence. No local organization provides an engineering-based custom device service at low cost. Until now.

Founder
Aidra, 2026
The founder

A student who saw a gap and decided to fill it

I noticed that elderly neighbors in our community were struggling with small physical challenges — a grab bar that didn't fit, a ramp angle that was wrong, a handle their hands couldn't grip — and the right solution either didn't exist or cost hundreds of dollars.

"Engineering should serve everyone. Not just the people who can afford it."

I founded Aidra to bridge that gap using SolidWorks design and local manufacturing partnerships — delivering custom assistive devices to Forsyth County seniors and disabled residents at the lowest possible cost.

V
Varsha Sivakumar
Founder · Rising Senior · Mechanical Engineering
